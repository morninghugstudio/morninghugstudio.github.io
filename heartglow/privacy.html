Heartglow Privacy Policy

Effective date: 28 October 2026

Heartglow is made by MorningHug Studio (RAIPIRAB COMPANY LIMITED) ("we", "us").

The short version

Heartglow does not collect, sell or share your personal data. Everything you do in the app stays on your phone.

What the app stores, and where

To work, the app saves the following only on your device:

  • the promise you chose (1 minute or 3 breaths) and your reminder time
  • the dates and lengths of your sits
  • the wisdom cards you collected and saved
  • your chosen breathing rhythm, bell sound, background sound and Chakra Journey length

We have no server and no account system, so we cannot see this information. Deleting the app, or clearing its storage, deletes it permanently.

Notifications

If you allow it, the app schedules one daily reminder on your phone. Reminders are created by the app on your device; nothing is sent to us.

Purchases

Heartglow Plus subscriptions are handled entirely by Google Play. We do not receive your payment card details. Google Play shares with us only what is needed to confirm that your subscription is active. Google's privacy policy applies to that processing: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Feedback you send us

If you choose to send feedback from the app, it opens your own email app. We then receive only what you put in that email (your email address, your message and the app version), and we use it only to reply and to improve Heartglow.

Analytics, ads and tracking

Heartglow contains no ads, no analytics SDKs and no tracking.

Children

Heartglow is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
