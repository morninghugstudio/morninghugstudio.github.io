Heartglow Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 November 2026

Heartglow is made by MorningHug Studio (RAIPIRAB COMPANY LIMITED) ("we", "us"). This policy covers Heartglow on Android (Google Play) and on iPhone (the App Store).

The short version

Heartglow does not collect, sell or share your personal data. Everything you do in the app stays on your phone.

What the app stores, and where

To work, the app saves the following only on your device:

  • the promise you chose (1 minute or 3 breaths) and your reminder time
  • the dates and lengths of your sits
  • the wisdom cards you collected and saved
  • your chosen breathing rhythm, bell sound, background and home sounds, and Chakra Journey length

We have no server and no account system, so we cannot see this information. Deleting the app, or clearing its storage, deletes it permanently.

If your phone backs up apps (Android’s backup to your Google account, or iCloud / computer backup on iPhone), this information may be included in that backup, so it can come back when you set up a new phone. That backup is kept in your Google or Apple account under their terms; we cannot see it. You can turn it off in your phone’s backup settings.

Notifications

If you allow it, the app schedules reminders on your phone: the daily reminder at the time you choose and, if you start a free trial, one reminder before the trial ends. Reminders are created by the app on your device; nothing is sent to us.

Purchases

Heartglow Plus subscriptions are handled entirely by Google Play (on Android) or the App Store (on iPhone). We do not receive your payment card details. Google or Apple share with us only what is needed to confirm that your subscription is active. Their privacy policies apply to that processing: Google https://policies.google.com/privacy · Apple https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

Ratings

The app may show the store’s own rating window (Google Play or the App Store). Your rating and review go to the store, not to us, and the app is not told whether you rated it.

Feedback you send us

If you choose to send feedback from the app, it opens your own email app. We then receive only what you put in that email (your email address, your message and the app version), and we use it only to reply and to improve Heartglow.

Analytics, ads and tracking

Heartglow contains no ads, no analytics SDKs and no tracking.

Children

Heartglow is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
