Privacy Policy — Idle Boyfriend

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Idle Boyfriend ("the game") is published by MorningHug Studio, a game label of Raipirab Co., Ltd., Thailand ("we", "us").

What we collect

We do not ask for, collect or store your name, email or any account. Your game progress is saved only on your device.

Advertising

The game offers optional rewarded ads (you choose to watch an ad for an in-game reward). Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information from your device, such as the advertising ID, IP address (which can indicate your approximate location), device and app performance information, and ad interactions. This information is sent encrypted. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy Choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

How Google uses this information: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

The game does not use any other analytics or tracking services.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by Google Play. We do not receive your name, email or payment details. The game receives only a purchase confirmation (purchase token) from Google Play so it can deliver your item.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 18 and over and is not directed at children.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the game deletes all game data from your device. Advertising data held by Google is governed by Google's privacy policy.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page and update the date above.
